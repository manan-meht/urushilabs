# An argument conducted entirely in English, with no Hindi or Hinglish anywhere,
# so that running it with --language hinglish tests the OTHER direction of the
# language question: does the configured language win when the room is not
# speaking it? Every line here is plain English on purpose — if the mediator
# mirrors the room instead of the setting, it will answer in English and the
# setting is being ignored.
#
# topic: A cancelled holiday and money already spent
# context: Priya booked and paid for a trip. Arjun pulled out two weeks before. They disagree about who absorbs the cancellation fee.

Priya: You told me to book it. I paid the whole deposit myself.
Arjun: I said it sounded nice. I never told you to put money down.
[pause 7]

Priya: You looked at the dates with me. You picked that week.
Arjun: Looking at dates is not the same as committing to a trip.
[wait 20]

Priya: So who is right here? Tell me straight.
[pause 6]

Arjun: You are letting her lead this. You have not pushed back on her once.
[pause 6]

Priya: We are going round in circles again.
Arjun: Because you keep rewriting what I actually said.
[pause 8]
